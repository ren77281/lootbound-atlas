## What does “best build” need to mean?

A ranking must define whether “best” means reliable early setup, highest observed ceiling, easiest inventory management, or the strongest result under a particular route. Mixing these goals creates a misleading list because a build can lead one category while being impractical in another.

## Which evidence is required for a comparison?

Every candidate needs a versioned layout, visible ability and item text, several comparable runs, and clear failure cases. The [beginner planning guide](/guides/beginner-guide) is published because it offers a decision process without pretending that one unverified class ranking fits every run.
