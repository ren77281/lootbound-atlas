## What evidence does a Bard and Jester comparison need?

Both sides must be tested under the same game version with their visible abilities, inventory layouts, and route context recorded. Comparing one complete run with one incomplete setup would exaggerate the difference and turn the result into a story about drops rather than class behavior.

## How will the guide separate shared and unique choices?

The finished page will identify shared equipment roles first, then document the class-specific anchors and the cost of switching direction. Until those records exist, use the [companion planning guide](/mechanics/companion-system) for evaluating any run-defining choice without assuming a future payoff.
