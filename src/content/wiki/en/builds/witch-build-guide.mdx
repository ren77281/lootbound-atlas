## How will Witch interactions be tested?

One placement or effect will change at a time while the rest of the setup remains fixed. The record must include the visible ability text, complete inventory, game version, and before-and-after result. That method prevents several simultaneous changes from being misread as one confirmed interaction.

## Why is there no finished Witch loadout yet?

A build name without reproducible components would add no trustworthy value. The future page will publish only when the core engine, replacements, failure points, and route needs are documented. The [aura and adjacency method](/mechanics/item-aura-adjacency) explains the evidence standard used here.
