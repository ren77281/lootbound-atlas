## What must be verified before recommending a Warrior build?

The guide needs exact in-game skill wording, the inventory footprint of every recommended item, and repeated tests showing that the proposed pieces work together in the same version. Without that evidence, a named loadout could become misinformation after one balance update or rely on an interaction that was only assumed.

## How will the finished build be evaluated?

The published version will identify a core engine, flexible replacements, the space cost of the setup, and the route conditions that make a pivot reasonable. Until then, use the general [equipment synergy framework](/items/early-equipment-synergies) instead of treating this draft as a recommendation.
