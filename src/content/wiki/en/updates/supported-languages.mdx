## How many languages did Lootbound support at launch?

ArtDock announced manual multilingual support for 19 languages at Lootbound's launch. The list covers English, major European and Asian languages, several Southeast Asian languages, and regional variants for Portuguese and Spanish. The developer described these as manually supported languages rather than automatic machine translation.

## Does the announcement guarantee every localization detail?

No. The official post confirms the 19-language launch list but does not document subtitle coverage, voice acting, font support, translation updates, or whether every interface element uses the selected language. Those details should be checked in the current Steam store listing and game build.

## Why are the regional labels important?

The announcement specifically names Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, and Latin American Spanish. Those labels are more precise than saying only Chinese, Portuguese, or Spanish, because they identify the language variants the developer chose to list for launch.

The post calls the overall work manual multilingual support. It does not identify the translators, explain the review process, or promise identical terminology across future patches, so this page does not infer those details.

## Where can I verify the official language list?

Read ArtDock's [official 19-language announcement on Steam](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1836506165576194), published on July 6, 2026. The list above follows the names and order used in that announcement.
