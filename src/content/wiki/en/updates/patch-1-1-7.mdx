## What changed in Lootbound Patch 1.1.7?

Patch 1.1.7 changes item selection so players can choose between one common reward and a dice roll with a chance of a better result. The update also makes Difficulty II slightly harder, fixes several minor bugs, and improves overall game stability, according to the developer's official notes.

| System | Official change |
| --- | --- |
| Item selection | Made more fair and predictable |
| Safe choice | Take one common reward |
| Risk choice | Roll the dice for a chance at something better |
| Difficulty II | Slightly increased challenge |
| General quality | Minor bug fixes and stability improvements |

<Callout type="info" title="The odds are not listed">Patch 1.1.7 does not publish the chance of receiving a better item, the possible reward pool, or the exact Difficulty II modifier changes.</Callout>

## How does the new reward choice work?

The update creates an explicit safe-versus-risk decision. The common reward is the predictable option described by the notes; rolling the dice gives up that certainty for a chance at something better. The announcement does not say that the risky choice always improves the reward or disclose its probability.

This makes route and reward decisions easier to describe without pretending the underlying odds are known. Use the [dice-roll mechanics guide](/mechanics/dice-roll-mechanics) for the confirmed boundaries, and the [map routes guide](/guides/map-routes-risk-reward) when comparing a reliable reward with a higher-variance option.

## Why was Difficulty II changed?

ArtDock says Difficulty II was made slightly more challenging so it better matches the intended difficulty progression. No enemy values, percentages, or named modifiers were included, so older and newer Difficulty II observations should be labeled with their game version before being compared.

The Difficulty II adjustment is separate from the item-selection change. The official announcement does not claim that choosing the common reward or rolling the dice changes the selected difficulty.
