## What will the Patch 1.1.5 page contain?

The final page will preserve the developer's stated changes, group them by affected system, and link each change to any guide that needs retesting. It will not infer hidden balance effects from wording alone or copy an unattributed community summary as though it were an official source.

## How will existing guides be reviewed?

Each published guide will be checked for claims related to the patch, then its `lastModified` and `gameVersion` fields will change only after review. The [achievement tracker](/guides/achievement-guide) shows the same preference for incomplete but verifiable records over guessed details.
