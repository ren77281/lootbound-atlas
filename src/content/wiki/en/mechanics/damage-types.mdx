## What must a Lootbound damage reference verify?

The source record needs every visible damage label, where it appears, and how the game describes related mitigation or conversion. Each interaction must be tested separately in one version because a patch can change terminology or behavior while an older community table continues circulating unchanged.

## How will conflicting damage claims be handled?

Official wording establishes the feature, direct tests check behavior, and community reports identify cases worth reproducing. Conflicts remain marked unresolved until one controlled comparison explains them. The [aura testing guide](/mechanics/item-aura-adjacency) documents the same one-variable evidence method.
