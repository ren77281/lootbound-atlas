## How should you prepare for the Lootbound Guardian?

Plan backward from the final setup. As soon as the run reveals a plausible Guardian direction, identify what it still needs and which existing equipment remains useful if the plan changes. This makes the end state a constraint throughout the run rather than a separate decision at the finish.

## How do you judge a Guardian part?

Measure each part by what it contributes to the whole setup, not by how impressive it looks alone. Include the inventory or route opportunity that must be given up to take it.

- Does the part reinforce the current plan or demand a pivot?
- What space or route opportunity does it consume?
- Does it provide immediate value, final value, or both?
- Can the rest of the build cover its weakness?

The [companion system guide](/mechanics/companion-system) uses the same whole-package evaluation for another run-defining choice.

## What should you check before the final encounter?

Reduce speculative pieces and confirm that the inventory still expresses one coherent plan. The goal is not to carry the largest collection of interesting parts; it is to arrive with a complete setup whose interactions and failure points you understand.

## Which details remain unverified?

This page does not claim a complete part list, exact boss phases, damage breakpoints, or a universal best Guardian. Those sections require screenshots and reproducible observations from the current build, and they will be versioned when added.
