## How should you evaluate a Lootbound dice choice?

List the outcomes shown by the game, the value of each outcome to the current build, and the cost of failure. If the interface does not disclose exact probabilities, do not silently assume every result has the same chance. Uncertainty can be managed without pretending that unknown odds are known.

## What belongs in a risk budget?

A risk budget asks whether the setup can afford the worst visible result and whether the upside solves an important problem. It keeps the decision tied to the current run instead of a desire to recover from the previous roll.

- Can the run continue after the worst visible result?
- Does the best result solve a meaningful bottleneck?
- Is there a lower-variance route to the same goal?
- Are you rolling because it is correct, or because the last roll failed?

Compare the available branches with the [route planning checklist](/guides/map-routes-risk-reward).

## Why is the result not the same as decision quality?

A successful roll does not prove the choice was good, and a failed roll does not automatically make it a mistake. Preserve the information available at decision time. This prevents short streaks from becoming false rules and makes the review useful across many runs.

## What would verify an exact probability?

A trustworthy probability claim needs a visible version, a clear event definition, repeated observations, and raw counts. Until that evidence exists, this site describes decision frameworks instead of publishing precise percentages that could mislead players.
