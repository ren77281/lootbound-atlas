<Callout type="info" title="Verification status">Companions are an official Lootbound feature. Named effects and exact values remain excluded until they can be captured and reproduced in the current build.</Callout>

## How should you choose a Lootbound companion?

Give the companion one clear job: reinforce the build's engine, cover a weakness, improve consistency, or open a better route. If the job cannot be stated in one sentence, the choice is probably being evaluated as an isolated bonus instead of part of the full run.

## How do you evaluate the full companion package?

Compare what changes immediately with what the companion demands from future rewards. A modest option that works now can be stronger than a theoretical upgrade that requires several missing pieces.

- What changes as soon as the companion joins?
- Which equipment becomes better or worse?
- Does the companion need space, adjacency, or a specific route?
- Does it solve the current bottleneck or only promise future value?

The [early equipment synergy guide](/items/early-equipment-synergies) provides a similar whole-package test for item pairs.

## How can you pivot without breaking the run?

Keep the pieces that still perform without special support, then replace the weakest non-synergistic item first. Test whether the new engine is actually stronger before giving it the remaining space. This creates a reversible pivot instead of dismantling a functional setup for a plan that may never arrive.

## What evidence should a companion entry contain?

A trustworthy entry needs the exact in-game wording, acquisition context, visible version, capture date, and a reproducible interaction test. Community reports are useful leads for deciding what to test, but repetition alone does not make a claimed effect confirmed.
