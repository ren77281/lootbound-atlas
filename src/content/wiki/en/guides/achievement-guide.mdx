## Which achievements unlock during normal progression?

Start by treating the boss and first-death achievements as natural progression rather than farming targets. **The First Fall** unlocks on the first failed run, while **Fall of the Bastion**, **The Fallen Queen**, and **Vizier's Oblivion** are tied to the three named boss defeats shown by Steam.

**No One Left Behind** is the first run-condition achievement worth planning around. It requires a completed run with no companion deaths, so it is safer to attempt after you understand how the AI-controlled party behaves and can maintain a stable inventory for every member. The [companion system guide](/mechanics/companion-system) explains the party-planning questions to check before committing to that attempt.

## Which achievements require separate hero clears?

Difficulty III completion is tracked separately for all four named heroes. Finishing with one hero does not satisfy the other three entries.

| Hero    | Difficulty III achievement |
| ------- | -------------------------- |
| Warrior | The Last General           |
| Bard    | Lamb of Rebellion          |
| Witch   | Witch of Catastrophe       |
| Paladin | Paladin of Purification    |

Build each clear around the hero you actually selected instead of forcing one universal inventory plan. The [inventory grid guide](/mechanics/inventory-grid-item-rotation) covers how to preserve enough board flexibility to change direction during a run.

## Which achievements should be progressed in the Hub?

Three achievements explicitly name Hub progression:

- **Facets of Fate** — buy every die in the Hub.
- **Alchemist's Apprentice** — buy 15 consumables in the Hub.
- **Trained Recruit** — buy every upgrade for one hero in the Barracks.

Avoid spreading resources only for the sake of making every counter move at once. Trained Recruit rewards completing a single hero's upgrade set, while Facets of Fate asks for the full dice catalog. If completion speed matters, pick one of those goals and check its remaining purchases before spending on unrelated unlocks.

## What are the long-term and companion achievements?

**Collector** requires 400 unique item discoveries, and **Titan Slayer** requires 50 elite kills. Both are cumulative goals, so ordinary varied runs should advance them without a dedicated one-run setup.

Four more achievements ask you to unlock every ability for a specific companion class:

- Warrior — **Master of Sword**
- Bard — **Spark of Inspiration**
- Witch — **Wisdom of Chaos**
- Paladin — **Fury of Faith**

The wording says “companion,” which is distinct from the four Difficulty III achievements that specify completing a run with the corresponding hero. Check the achievement name before investing resources so the hero-clear and companion-ability objectives are not confused.

## How should you attempt the 35-minute achievement?

Steam currently requires a completed run in **under 35 minutes** for **You Snooze, You Lose!**. Some third-party listings still show 45 minutes, but that does not match the current Steam requirement.

Use a separate speed attempt after you can already finish consistently. Reduce time spent rebuilding the same inventory, take a route you can evaluate quickly, and avoid testing unfamiliar interactions during the timed run. The timer condition only matters if the run is completed.

## What is the hidden Oops, Butterfingers! requirement?

Steam does not show the requirement on its global achievements page. A cross-platform achievement tracker lists **“Discard a blacksmith gem”** for the Xbox version, but this page does not treat that wording as confirmed for Steam because platform data can diverge. Use it as a test lead and verify whether Steam unlocks before relying on it.

  a reproducible Steam instruction.
